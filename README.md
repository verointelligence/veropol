## Overview

VERO reads public reporting from vetted outlets, keeps every item linked to its original publisher, and leaves the comparison to the reader. Reports are never rewritten or merged.

| Service | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Live** | Seven strategic theaters plus automatically detected developing stories, refreshed every five minutes. War theaters carry a live map. |
| **Explore** | Search any region, leader, company, game or topic in any language, with typed suggestions, related topics and timeframe, topic and region filters. |
| **Articles** | Signed analysis from independent adult authors, reviewed by an editor before publication, with profiles, reactions, threaded comments, follows and badges. |

## Features

**Monitoring**
- Seven fixed theaters in two groups: War Theaters (Ukraine, Middle East, Yemen and the Red Sea, Syria, Lebanon, Sudan) and Strategic Watch (Taiwan Strait, BRICS, Korean Peninsula, Sahel).
- Developing stories are detected from burst coverage across at least seven sources and removed automatically when the trend ends.
- Each theater shows its latest report, report volume, source count and a cover taken from the story itself.

**Search**
- Entities are recognized dynamically and classified as Leader, Person, Country, Place, Organization, Company, Game, Software, Market, Event or Topic.
- Adaptive relevance: strict match first, then broader matches, then related coverage.
- When the vetted sources hold little on a topic, Explore adds reports from the wider web, labeled **Web**.
- Related-topic suggestions help follow a story from one angle to the next.

**Community**
- Accounts from age 13. Reading, reacting, commenting and following are open to everyone. Publishing is limited to adults.
- Threaded replies and mentions with autocomplete.
- Public profiles, followers, and a badge system: Official, Verified, Subject Expert, Editor's Pick, Early Member and Published Author.
- Notifications for follows, new articles from followed authors, comments, replies, mentions, likes and announcements, with per-type settings and optional browser alerts.

**Interface**
- Light and dark themes that follow the system and can be switched instantly.
- Fully responsive layout with a centered navigation and active-page state.
- Seven languages, with a curated dictionary for interface text and machine translation for reports.

## Languages

English, Turkish, Arabic, French, Spanish, Russian and Chinese. Reports are machine-translated on request and always link to the original.

## Privacy

- Email addresses and dates of birth are never displayed publicly.
- Passwords are stored as salted hashes.
- An account and all of its content can be deleted at any time in the account settings.
